## Synthesis
- **Highest friction point:** The account gate, steps 5 to 10. That is 5 screens plus a trip to the mailbox, 385 s, or 47% of total time, to see a materials figure. Three things made it worse:
  - no data-use statement in the modal or on step 1/3;
  - the phone justified by "retrait de matériaux" when the user only wants a price;
  - about 10 s of silent loading on "Créer mon compte".
- **Highest emotional friction point:** Step 10, the e-mail confirmation screen. The user has given name, e-mail and phone, has waited and thought the form had failed, and still has no price. She says she is "tired and a bit cheated". Only "Votre devis est enregistré" kept her going. Step 8 (required phone field) was the closest she came to abandoning.
- **Final decision:** She continues to the end and reaches the goal at step 12. She keeps the screenshot to show the mason, but does not treat the 2 202 € as a real price.
- **Emotional summary:** "I came in suspicious, and the drawing that showed 48 m² before I had typed anything didn't help. I answered the size questions without trouble, except the thickness, where I had to guess. Then came the account: they warned me, which I respect, but nobody told me what they would do with my e-mail, and when they asked for my phone 'pour le retrait de matériaux' I felt I was already buying something. I nearly stopped there. After the long wait and the trip to my mailbox I was tired. When the 2 200 € finally appeared I was relieved, and even more so when my calculator agreed with their tonnes. But I still don't know if the mason's work is included, what 'terrassement' is, or how old these prices are. I have a number, but not a price I believe."
- **Detected risks:**
  - Real users with this profile may **abandon at the phone field (step 8)** or at the e-mail confirmation (step 10), and the run did not observe how long delivery takes or where the link lands.
  - **The 2 202 € may be read as an all-inclusive price**, or dismissed entirely, because "materials only, labour and delivery excluded" is not stated next to the figure.
  - **Pre-filled values in the 3D caption** (48 m², 36 m², 3,6 m³ while fields are empty) make users believe the site is computing with numbers they never gave.
  - **The thickness hint gives no guidance for a garage**, so the result rests on a guess.
  - **Wording mismatches lower trust**, and for this profile trust does not recover easily:
    - "Votre devis est enregistré" vs "Enregistrez ce devis";
    - the phone being "pour le retrait" vs "pour le fonctionnement du compte";
    - "à des fins commerciales" in the CGU.
  - **Unexplained jargon:** treillis, hérisson, ferraillage, terrassement.
  - **No date on the prices.**
  - **Rude or team-signed reviews on the home page** weaken credibility from the first screen. This is a content-moderation issue, not dummy data.
- **Structural insight:** The calculator itself works: one question per screen, units shown, a clear recap, and an honest warning before the account gate. Trust is lost in two places:
  - **Before the price:** an account and personal data are required without saying why they are needed and what they are used for.
  - **After the price:** the figure is shown without saying what it covers. The explanation that makes it credible is hidden behind an accordion.

  For a skeptical user the price must carry its own scope and basis. The account gate must explain itself where the data is asked for, not only in the CGU.
- **Fix this first:** On /resultats, put a permanent scope line right under "Votre dalle coûtera environ 2 202,00 €". Suggested wording: "Matériaux uniquement (béton, treillis, gravier), prix TTC relevés le JJ/MM/AAAA. Main-d'œuvre du maçon, livraison et terrassement non compris." Also show the key ratios (2,4 t/m³, 5 kg/m²) without needing to open "Comment est fait le calcul ?".
